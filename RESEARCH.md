# Wymore Inc. rebuild research

Line of business, confirmed from the live site (https://wymoreinc.com/) and the logo on every page: machining, welding, and industrial supplies. The shop also repairs, fabricates, and stocks metal, driveline parts, pumps, hardware, and tools. Tagline on the home page: "If it's metal, we can fix it, make it or get it!" Established line: "Serving Imperial Valley Since 1947." El Centro, CA.

Palette and type taken from the published pages, not from the peers: header and footer `rgb(0, 0, 255)`, nav khaki `rgb(202, 202, 172)` / `#cacaca`, page canvas `#f2f2f2`, text `#333` and white, fonts Arvo (wordmark), Courgette (the "since 1947" line), and Carme (navigation and body). The call button uses the yellow of the stand in the shop's own Apollo parachute tester photograph.

## Peer sites

Layout ideas only. No text, photos, or branding copied.

### J.R. Smith Welding — https://www.jrsmithwelding.com/

Industrial welding and machining shop (since 1986), with in-shop and in-field repair. Borrowed:

- A short trust strip of real facts immediately under the opening photograph (years, certified welding, license), instead of a slogan floating in a header.
- Services grouped into scannable clusters with a phone call as the main action, not a search box.
- Field work and shop work kept as two readings of the same list (the live site already says line boring is "in-house or on site").

### Thompson Repairs — https://www.thompsonrepairs.com/

Custom metal fabrication, precision machining, and industrial welding (Jacksonville). Borrowed:

- The photograph sits uncovered, and the trade line plus the "since" year sit below it.
- The first content block is the three trades the logo already names: machining, welding, and supplies.
- A short "who we are" passage (the live about paragraph) comes before the long process lists.

### Industrial Weld & Repair, Inc. — https://iwr-inc.net/

Family industrial welding, fabrication, machining, and field repair (Clayton, NJ). Borrowed:

- Real shop and job photographs in a grid, opened in a cross-fade lightbox, instead of stock art.
- Capability groups as tight lists (shop processes beside products and supplies), matching the live two-column services page.
- Certifications shown only as small labels for claims the live site actually makes ("AWS certified", CSLB# 1020734).
